# Source documents

The PDFs in this folder are deliberately not committed. This manifest is how a
reader locates them. Titles are given as printed on each document.

## CIAF final report on the Santiago de Compostela derailment

- **File:** `IF_240713_200514_CIAF.pdf`
- **Title:** INFORME FINAL SOBRE EL ACCIDENTE GRAVE FERROVIARIO Nº 0054/2013 OCURRIDO EL DÍA 24.07.2013 EN LAS PROXIMIDADES DE LA ESTACIÓN DE SANTIAGO DE COMPOSTELA (A CORUÑA)
- **Reference:** IF-240713-200514-CIAF
- **Issued by:** Comisión de Investigación de Accidentes Ferroviarios, Ministerio de Fomento
- **Date:** 20 May 2014 (printed "Madrid, 20 de mayo de 2014", p. 111)
- **Length and language:** 266 pp., Spanish
- **Public URL:** https://www.era.europa.eu/system/files/2023-07/IF_240713_200514_CIAF.pdf (hosted by the European Union Agency for Railways)
- **Relied on by:** The Handoff, for the opening story and the death toll.

- **Authors:** James M. Shultz, Maria Paz Garcia-Vera, Clara Gesteira Santos, Jesus Sanz, George Bibel, Carl Schulman, George Bahouth, Yasmin Dias Guichot, Zelde Espinel and Andreas Rechkemmer
